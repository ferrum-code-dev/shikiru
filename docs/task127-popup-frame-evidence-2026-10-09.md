## Behavior

The shared `PopupFrame` captures its preferred outer size after meaningful initial content is ready. Its measurement includes virtual child extents, the authored maximum width and fixed chrome so a popup opened in a constrained viewport can recover its purpose-appropriate preference later. Loading placeholders are not initial content. History pagination, filters, live content and expanded dropdowns preserve the captured size and centered position; terminal resize clamps to the available frame while retaining the preference, then restores it when the viewport grows. A new popup content purpose may reset the capture; page navigation and data refresh do not.

The popup title, `Esc ×`, separating rule and fixed route controls remain inside the visible frame and outside its scrolling body. Palette drafts, text focus and caret, selected task identity, body reading position and originating context remain in the mounted or standalone route. Deferred sizing and readiness callbacks first confirm that their widget remains attached. This prevents a queued callback from querying `screen` after its popup has closed, even when Textual still reports the removed widget as mounted.

The real SQLite/Textual route `tests/integration/test_dashboard_task_detail.py::test_task_detail_views_mouse_opens_task_actions_and_help` retains actual Palette, Help, TaskDetail and PopupFrame references, closes those screens, then replays their sizing/readiness callbacks through the active app. It verifies that Home remains active, the selected task identity remains available, and Help can still be opened and closed.
